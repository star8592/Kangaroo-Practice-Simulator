## Dynamic mixed mocks

Six grade-specific mixed generators are available:

- Grades 1–2: 24 questions, 8×3 + 8×4 + 8×5, currently mixing Portugal and Austria.
- Grades 3–4: 24 questions, currently mixing Austria and Germany.
- Grades 5–6: 24 questions, currently mixing Austria and Germany.
- Grades 7–8: 30 questions, 10×3 + 10×4 + 10×5, Austria + Germany.
- Grades 9–10: 30 questions, Austria + Germany.
- Grades 11–13: 30 questions, Austria + Germany.

The generator balances difficulty, year, region, source paper and original question position. A seed reproduces the same mixed paper.

## Student-language gate

Student-facing content is permitted only when it can be completed in Chinese or English. Portuguese/German/French source language may remain in private provenance metadata, but must not be required to solve an exposed exam.

English official papers can be released directly as `EN`. Reviewed localized papers are released as `中 / EN`.
