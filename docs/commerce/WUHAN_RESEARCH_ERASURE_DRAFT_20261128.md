# Research report erasure and replay protection (draft)
Based on R73 SOC THINK 855d48d. This separate candidate addresses archive privacy before production onboarding. An authorized internal erasure service must derive the owner from an authenticated, verified request; caller-supplied owner values are not authentication. No public deletion endpoint, no automatic TTL, no production migration or grant. The research archive remains nonbillable and does not store original audio.

A transaction deletes the owner-scoped report, then stores a tombstone containing only the opaque random attempt identifier and erasure timestamp. Both archive and erase operations acquire the same transaction advisory lock. Late signed delivery or retry cannot resurrect the deleted archive, including a delivery with another owner. An unknown attempt or incorrect owner cannot create a tombstone. Eraser, reader and writer functions have distinct intended grants; table privileges remain revoked. The result for an erased/unknown/wrong-owner report is not_found on an erasure attempt. The archive writer returns the existing generic conflict result, not private erasure metadata.

Real PostgreSQL 18 disposable local-socket fixture passed owner scope, repeated erase, late and reassigned replay, minimal tombstone, role separation, and overlapping erase/late archive. No real user account or report involved. Test creates its own temporary cluster, no external DB URL accepted. Before production, establish verified parent/guardian ownership, actual account erasure request workflow, erasure of scorer-side persisted reports and temporary media, backup expiry and restoration controls, legally appropriate retention and tombstone lifetime, and audit policy. A database archive deletion alone is NOT end-to-end personal data erasure. Tombstones may remain linkable through other systems and must be included in the retention review. Do not grant erasure to the billing service or enable cleanup automatically.
