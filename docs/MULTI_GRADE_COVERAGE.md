## Mixed exams

Each grade band has an independent deterministic mixed-paper pool.
The generator balances difficulty, year, source exam, source position, and country where multiple countries are available.

Current cross-region mixes for Grades 3–13 draw evenly from Austria and Germany. Grades 1–2 currently mix Portugal and Austria.
